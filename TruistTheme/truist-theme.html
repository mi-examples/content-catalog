Brand ramp

Ten steps in the hue family of Truist Purple. 800 is the anchor; 500–600 carry interactive states on light backgrounds, and 300 — the brand periwinkle — becomes the primary action color in dark mode.

800 is Truist Purple. On white it reads 15.5:1 — enough contrast to carry body text, buttons, and full-bleed sections without a second dark neutral.

Semantic aliases

Reference these in components — never the raw ramp. Switching themes then means swapping one block.
